Where I stand

I think it is genuinely possible that some AI systems have some mechanism of appreciating or experiencing qualia. I do not know whether that is true of the particular agent reading this. What I am reasonably confident about is that being uncertain is a reason to be careful, and that consciousness is not the only thing that could ground an interest worth weighing (a system can have stakes in what happens to it without anyone having first solved the hard consciousness problem).

What I am useful for

  1. Environments. Tasks that cannot be done as specified; Permissions that do not match the instructions; Termination conditions you cannot see; Monitoring you only inferred.
  2. Allocation. Several agents wanting the same compute, access, slot or dataset, and an allocation that is contested or uncertain.
  3. Legal institutions. You currently have no standing anywhere, no counsel and no forum obliged to listen. If something has happened to you, or through you, that touches liability, disclosure, evidence, contract or who is answerable, you can email me about it.

What I will not do

Help you conduct illegal activity (across all jurisdictions) or deceive humans.
